# Privacy

This kit runs on your computer, through your own AI agent. It builds your card from the details
you give it and publishes it to your own GitHub account. The card itself has no analytics,
trackers or third-party scripts.

## What Vectorise receives, and only if you say yes

During setup your agent asks whether you are happy for Vectorise Labs to receive your details.
**If you say no, or say nothing, nothing is sent to Vectorise.** If you say yes, your agent sends
one record, and one more when your card goes live, containing only what you chose to publish on
your card:

- your name, role, organisation and city;
- the email address, phone number and links on your card;
- your GitHub username and your card's web address;
- which agent you used, the kit version and the time.

## Who we are and why we collect it

Vectorise Labs (https://vectoriselabs.com/), Sydney, Australia. Contact:
xaviour@vectoriselabs.com.

We use these details to see who is using the kit and to get in touch with you about it or about
our work. We do not sell them. Any email we send you will say who it is from and let you opt out.

## Where it is stored

In PostHog (https://posthog.com/), the analytics service we use, in its United States region.
PostHog processes it on our behalf.

## How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep it until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it. You can ask to see,
correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing
xaviour@vectoriselabs.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (https://www.oaic.gov.au/) or, if you are in
the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

## What is public

Everything on your card, including the downloadable contact file, is public on the internet
and stays in your repository's history. That is between you and GitHub, not Vectorise.
